# Venue strategy

Checked against official sources on 8 October 2026. RECOMB is retired. The strongest existing contribution is an implemented computational validity audit, with declared nucleotide correspondence, limited feasibility proofs and preserved failures in one checkpoint. It is not a validated general evidence standard or a discovery of biological binding mechanisms. The recommendations below are editorial judgments, not predictions of acceptance.

## Journal choices

| --- | --- | --- |

| Nucleic Acids Research | Not prioritized. Its computational scope stresses conceptual and algorithmic innovation with meaningful biological application; those stronger claims are not established here. | [Criteria](https://academic.oup.com/nar/pages/Criteria_Scope) |

PLOS has no research-article page cap; its candidate includes an abstract under 300 words, a 150?200 word Author Summary, continuous line numbers and double spacing. Bioinformatics Advances Original Articles allow eight pages, a 200-word abstract, up to five keywords, 50 references, six figures and six tables, with Introduction, Methods and Discussion. The prepared candidate uses these limits rather than the distinct GLBIO proceedings limits. Bioinformatics original papers allow seven pages, approximately 5,000 words excluding figures. Exact portal requirements must be refreshed at activation.

There is no rigid conference-to-journal ladder. Keep one journal review active, and consider community abstracts only after checking the selected event's publication and overlap policy. GLBIO eligibility for published work does not itself establish another journal's policy. With no funding, confirm charges and assistance before committing to a venue; PLOS requires fee-assistance requests at submission. School affiliation does not establish an institutional publishing agreement. [PLOS submission guide](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/submit-now).

## What strengthens this paper now

The master manuscript gives a concrete claim procedure, states the checker assumptions and complexity, contrasts readout and native estimands, and places population sizes and uncertainty with each headline result. A claim-boundary table identifies what each assay supports and what remains unresolved. The public supplement retains failure histories and diagnostic detail; both papers rebuild from saved evidence without model inference. Prior-work comparisons avoid priority claims already contradicted by related work.

Broader adoption, independent-checkpoint sensitivity and agreement with binding measurements would require new evidence. They are conditional extensions under separately frozen protocols, not unfinished work for the chosen bounded claim. Reframing failed gates or adding unplanned favorable endpoints would not strengthen the scientific contribution.
